Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

By Hand is a private tool run on the account owner's own computer. It connects only to the account's own Instagram professional account and Facebook Page.

What it accesses

  • Instagram content publishing: to post the account's own reels.
  • Instagram insights: to read view, like and comment counts of the account's own posts.
  • Instagram comments: to read comments left on the account's own posts and reply to them.

What it stores

The access token and the numbers it reads are stored only on the owner's computer. Comment text is used only to write a reply and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

To have any data about you removed, send a direct message to the By Hand Instagram account. Removing the app from your Facebook or Instagram settings also ends its access.

Contact

Direct message to the By Hand account on Instagram.
